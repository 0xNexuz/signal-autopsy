Product documentation

Signal Autopsy is the pre-execution safety harness for autonomous agents operating on Bitget Reality tokenized US equities.

What happens to an intent

  1. Reality ticker and candle evidence arrives through Agent Hub and UTA v3.
  2. Qwen adversarially examines the thesis but receives no execution authority.
  3. A deterministic engine scores session, liquidity, event, volatility, size, and overconfidence risk.
  4. The server emits a signed receipt with a route, notional cap, and expiry.
  5. The route gate verifies the receipt and fails closed on any mismatch.
  6. Failure Memory later evaluates whether the intervention was useful.

Truth labels

REAL is used for completed service calls or enforced server logic. DEMO marks a visible fallback. SIMULATED means the real policy ran but no exchange order was submitted. BLOCKED means a dependency or safety requirement was unavailable.

Signed receipts

The receipt binds the instrument, side, requested notional, market snapshot, six-component score, route, maximum notional, and expiry. Any alteration invalidates the signature. Receipts persist in the current browser because this build intentionally has no database.

Qwen boundary

Qwen may surface a countercase, assumptions, and evidence requests. The deterministic engine alone controls execution authorization. Removing Qwen or changing its response does not bypass the route gate.

Benchmark

The fixed-as-of benchmark compares the same historical Reality candle intents at full exposure and under Signal Autopsy's route multipliers. Candle data is REAL when loaded from Bitget; the generated intents and intervention policy are SIMULATED. Results are not a profitability forecast.
